What Fixate does

Fixate presents readable text from a web page or your selection in a reading overlay. Extraction depends on the page structure and browser permissions. Protected browser pages, the Chrome Web Store, PDF viewers and content inside embedded frames may not be supported. Reading speed and comprehension vary; no particular result is guaranteed.

Free trial and Pro

The current extension includes a 14-day Pro trial without requiring a payment card. When it ends, the free plan allows three qualifying articles per day. Texts under 150 words do not count. The daily count resets at local midnight, and reopening an article already counted that day does not use another allowance. Pro removes this daily article limit.

Where a recurring plan is purchased, charges recur as disclosed at checkout until cancelled. Payment and account-management services are provided through ExtensionPay and Stripe.
